EPIC RACER — couch night storyboard

Rough sketches of how the pieces fit together. Shapes and layout matter here, not polish.

1The setup. Laptop runs the game and plugs into the TV over HDMI. Phones join over home Wi-Fi. Xbox pads pair to the laptop.
2TV lobby. Big QR code and room code. Each player pops in as a coloured card the moment they join.
3Phone controller. Landscape. Left thumb drags to steer, right thumb owns big buttons. Header shows place, lap, held item and a Wi-Fi dot.
4Vehicle select. Chunky low-poly silhouettes you can name from the couch. Three stats, so trade-offs are obvious.
5Four-way split screen. Each quarter is a chunky low-res view with fog pop-in, PS1 style. Place and lap in the corner.
6Set piece: the Paris fold. The street hinges up and over. You keep racing on the underside of the city. Every track gets one moment like this.
